🔐 Security Proof

This page documents how Onyx Cloud handles your data. We do not store, sell, or share your personal information.

1. Password Hashing

Onyx Cloud uses PBKDF2-SHA512 with 100,000 iterations to hash passwords.

This is the same standard used by 1Password, LastPass, and other password managers.

Hash method: pbkdf2:sha512:100000
Format: pbkdf2:SALT:HASH
Storage: data/users.json (encrypted, not readable)

We never store your password in plain text. We cannot read your password.

2. What We Store

  • Email — for login and verification
  • Password hash — not the password itself
  • Chat history — to improve AI quality
  • Account tier — free, basic, premium, pro

3. What We Do NOT Store

  • Your real name
  • Your location (unless you explicitly allow it)
  • Your contacts
  • Your files
  • Your API keys (they are stored locally on your device)

4. Data Access

We do not access your data. Our server only processes what you send through the chat.

If you want proof, you can:

  • Check data/users.json in our GitHub repo — it only contains email and password hash
  • Check data/chats/ — it only contains your chat messages
  • Ask us for a data export — we will provide everything we have

5. No Third Parties

We do not use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising.

We do not share your data with anyone.
